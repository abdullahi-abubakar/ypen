Who We Are

A Generation Rising
for Lasting Change

YESN — the Youth Empowerment and Sustainable Network — is a grassroots organization mobilizing young Nigerians and Africans to take bold action on the world's most pressing challenges.

We operate at the intersection of advocacy, education, community health, and sustainable development — equipping youth with the tools, networks, and platforms they need to drive meaningful impact aligned with the United Nations' 2030 Agenda.

Our work is grounded in the belief that young people are not just the leaders of tomorrow — they are the changemakers of today.

🌍

Our Mission

Mobilize youth to advance human rights, sustainability, and inclusive development.

🎯

Our Vision

A world where every young person has the agency to build a just, clean, and equitable future.

🤝

Our Approach

Community-centered, evidence-based, and deeply collaborative programs.

⚡

Our Values

Integrity, inclusion, innovation, and relentless commitment to human dignity.

What We Do

Our Five
Focus Areas

01 ⚖️

Human Rights Advocacy

We amplify youth voices on issues of justice, equality, and civil liberties — engaging communities, governments, and institutions to uphold and protect fundamental human rights for all.

02 📚

Education & Health Support

We deliver programs that improve access to quality education and healthcare — running literacy initiatives, health awareness campaigns, and connecting underserved youth to critical resources.

03 🚀

Youth Empowerment

We build capacity through skills training, leadership development, mentorship, and entrepreneurship programs — enabling young people to become confident agents of change in their communities.

04 🌱

Clean Energy

We advocate for renewable energy access and climate action — educating communities on sustainable energy solutions, championing clean energy policy, and mobilizing youth for a green future.

05 🌾

Agriculture & Food Security

We equip youth with modern farming knowledge, agri-entrepreneurship skills, and sustainable land-use practices — mobilizing young Nigerians to drive food security and rural economic growth across communities.
